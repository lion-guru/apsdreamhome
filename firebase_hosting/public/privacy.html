Welcome to APS Dream Home ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. 1. Information We Collect

When you use the APS Dream Home application, we collect information that helps deliver real estate booking and partner services:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, mobile phone number, email address, password, residential address, and profile photo when registering as a customer, associate, or employee.
  • KYC & Documentation: Government-issued ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN), property allotment records, and payment receipts submitted voluntarily for plot allotment, registry documentation, and associate KYC verification.
  • Transaction & Booking Details: Plot selections, colony choices, token payment amounts, EMI schedules, transaction reference numbers, and commission payout details.
  • Location Information: Approximate location accessed only when you actively search for nearby colonies, projects, or calculate route distances to project sites. Background location is NOT accessed.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs to ensure app performance and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate business operations:

  • To manage real estate plot searches, reservations, and digital allotment documentation.
  • To calculate and disburse associate partner sales commissions, referral bonuses, and genealogy downline metrics.
  • To track EMI payments and send timely receipt confirmations and milestone alerts.
  • To verify customer and partner identity in compliance with applicable RERA and Indian regulatory guidelines.
  • To provide customer support and respond to property inquiries via phone, email, or WhatsApp.

3. Device Permissions & Rationale

Transparency Pledge: We request only the minimum necessary permissions required for core real estate transactions.
  • Camera & Photo/Media Library: Used solely when you choose to take or upload a photo of your KYC document, payment receipt, or profile image. No background photo access is performed.
  • Storage / Media Files: Used to download and save your official digital allotment letter, payment receipts, colony layout maps, and rate sheets to your device.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver real-time updates regarding EMI due dates, payment acknowledgments, and associate commission credits. You can disable notifications anytime in system settings.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only under strict conditions:

  • Authorized Associates: Assigned telecallers or associates assisting you with your specific plot booking or site visit.
  • Payment Gateways: Secure, RBI-compliant payment processors for processing plot tokens and EMI payments.
  • Legal Compliance: Government authorities, RERA regulators, or law enforcement bodies when strictly required by applicable law or judicial orders.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ enterprise-grade security standards to safeguard your data:

  • All communications between the mobile app and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL).
  • Sensitive documents and payment records are stored in protected databases with role-based access controls and regular security auditing.
  • No financial credentials (such as net banking passwords, UPI PINs, or CVVs) are ever stored on our servers.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill business purposes or comply with legal property registration standards. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and personal data by contacting our Data Protection Officer at techguruabhay@gmail.com. Upon verification, requested data will be permanently purged within 30 days, subject to statutory record-keeping requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our real estate services and application are intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
