LifeKit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

LifeKit ("the app") is a personal daily-life companion — notes, tasks, habit tracker, expense tracker, routine planner, tools and AI assistant. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • LifeKit does not require an account, sign-up, or login.
  • LifeKit does not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, or any personal identifiers.
  • Your notes, tasks, expenses, habits and routines are stored only on your device (local storage). They never leave your phone.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data — because we do not collect it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use:

  • Device or other identifiers (e.g., advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interactions with ads (impressions, clicks)
  • Diagnostics related to ad delivery

You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device. For details, see Google's privacy documentation: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Third-Party Services

  • dummyjson.com — fetches a random motivational quote (no personal data sent).
  • api.open-meteo.com — fetches current weather for a default city (no personal data sent).

Both services receive only a network request; no personal information is transmitted.

4. Permissions

  • INTERNET — used for ads, weather, and quotes.
  • No other permissions (camera, contacts, storage, GPS) are used.

5. Children's Privacy

LifeKit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Data Deletion

All app data lives locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all data permanently. There is nothing stored on our servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
